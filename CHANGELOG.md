# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- First version.
- The window opens by itself as soon as a group roll starts and closes a few seconds after the last roll has a winner (it stays open while the mouse is over it).
- Plain, small window. For every item: who picked Need, Greed, Disenchant or Pass, who is still choosing, every roll and the winner – live.
- Big Need / Greed / Pass buttons under the item while you still have to roll (can be turned off).
- Finished rolls stay in the list per character, also after `/reload`. A Clear button empties the list.
- The list is cleared on a new dungeon or raid run: another dungeon, another group, or after a reset message (setting, on by default).
- Loot history (note button, top left, or `/lootrolls history`): every item ever rolled on, newest first and grouped by dungeon run (also the same dungeon twice in a row), raid or zone visit. It stays when you clear the list or enter a new dungeon. Filters for your own wins and for item quality (e.g. from rare, to hide green items).
- Loot history: only the newest dungeon run is open, older ones are folded in. A button at the bottom collapses or expands all of them at once.
- `/lootrolls test` shows made-up demo rolls to try the window.
- Settings under Options → AddOns → Forever Loot Rolls.
- English and German.
